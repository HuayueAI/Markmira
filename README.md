## ✨ Explore Markmira

### 01 · WYSIWYG Editor — *Just Write. See the Result.*

A seamless editing experience without switching between Markdown source and preview.

### 02 · Multi-format Workspace — *Different Formats. One Workspace.*

Work with Markdown, PDFs, spreadsheets, and more in one place.

### 03 · AI Revision — *Let AI Suggest. You Decide.*

Refine, rewrite, and polish your writing while staying in control of every change.

### 04 · Local-first — *Your Files. Your Rules.*

Keep your documents on your device in portable, open formats.

### 05 · Plugin Ecosystem — *As Light as You Want. As Powerful as You Need.*

Start with a lightweight Markdown editor and extend your workspace with plugins. Add only what you need, and make Markmira truly yours.

Official plugins — **AI Assistant**, **Draw.io**, **Excalidraw**, **PDF Viewer** and **Text Editor** — are distributed from the [markmira-plugin-releases](https://github.com/HuayueAI/markmira-plugin-releases) repository and can be installed, updated and removed from the built-in plugin market inside the app.
